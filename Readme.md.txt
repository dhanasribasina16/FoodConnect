# FOODCONNECT-To-Supply-Leftover-Food-to-Poor
FoodConnect is a Salesforce-based application designed to bridge the gap between food donors and recipients. Built entirely on the Salesforce platform, the project leverages custom objects, automation tools. The goal is to reduce food waste and streamline the logistics of food distribution through a centralized and scalable system.
------
**Project Overview**
FoodConnect is a Salesforce-powered platform designed to reduce food wastage while addressing hunger in underserved communities. The system connects surplus food providers such as restaurants, supermarkets, and event organizers with NGOs, shelters, and individuals in need.
